Worked solution

Equation numbers follow the lecture slides. Underlined numbers are your inputs; hover a row to find it on the drawing.

Assumptions in this model

  • The overburden block is a parallelogram W wide and D high whose sides follow the highwall angle β, so its area is W × D. Everything is per 1 m of strip length (L = 1).
  • The spoil of each cut fills a W-wide strip of the previous pit. Its cross-section is the parallelogram W × H minus the triangle between neighbouring peaks, (W/2)² tanθ.
  • The tub set-back PD is taken as 0.75 × TD as a safety allowance. TD is only known once a machine is chosen, so treat it as a trial value.
  • Following the lecture solution, ore that is uncovered but lost (OLoss) is converted to bank m³ with the ore density and added to the dragline's annual volume.
  • The lecture solution rounds intermediate values (5.88 Mt/yr, 6,360 h/yr). This page carries full precision, so with Example 2 loaded BC shows 74.7 m³ rather than 74.63 m³, and MSL about 192,600 kg rather than 192,426 kg.
  • The dragline outline, boom angle and bucket are drawn schematically to show where the boom point must reach. Only OR, RF and PD are computed. The bucket drawing is scaled to the computed bucket capacity.
  • The 120° swing angle in Example 2 does not enter these equations.
