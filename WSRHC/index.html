Reference: Hawley M. and Cunning J., (2017), “Guidelines for Mine Waste Dump and Stockpile Design”, CRC Press, 368 pages.

WSRHC interactive module · Rating tables follow Tables 3.3–3.11 and level of effort follows Table 3.12 of Hawley & Cunning (2017), Chapter 3 (M. Hawley). The WSRHC is a screening and classification tool; it does not replace site-specific investigation, stability analysis or the judgement of a qualified geotechnical specialist.
