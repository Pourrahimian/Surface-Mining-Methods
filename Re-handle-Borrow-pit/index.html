Schematic, approximately to scale: 25 m overburden over a 4 m seam, 65° highwalls, 45° end face, 37° spoil repose, 95 m boom at 32°. A shallow borrow pit (8 m deep, 56 m wide at the top) was cut first, parallel to the box cut on its waste side, and its waste was set aside to the east, away from the box cut. The dragline stands at the end of the box cut and swings about 90° to cast the box-cut overburden into the borrow pit. The borrow pit is sized to hold the swelled box-cut spoil within the dragline’s reach and dump height. The violet outline marks the next cut, and the amber outline marks the borrow pit in Section A–A, which looks north, with east on the right.
