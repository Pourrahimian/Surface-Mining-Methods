Cross-section

Vertical: the azimuth is the strike of the section line (090° is an east–west section). The part of the mine in front of the plane is cut away. Bench plan cuts horizontally at a bench elevation.

Reserve & grade–tonnage

The reserve is the in-situ blocks between the current pit and the ultimate pit shell. Blue: tonnage above cutoff. Orange: average grade above cutoff. The red line is the current cutoff.

Layers & tools

Measure: click two points on the ground to get the distance, the height difference and the grade (for example along the ramp).

This is a synthetic porphyry copper deposit, approximately to scale. Blocks are 20 × 20 × 10 m. Benches are 10 m high with 70° faces and 9 m berms. The haul ramp is 26 m wide at 10 % grade, with a switchback.
