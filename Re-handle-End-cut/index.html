Schematic, approximately to scale: 25 m overburden over a 4 m seam, 65° highwalls, 45° end face, 37° spoil repose, 95 m boom at 32°. The dragline stands at the end of the box cut, in line with it, and casts the overburden to both sides with a swing of about 90° each way. The pile on the line of advance (the west pile here) has to be re-handled later to the spoil side (east). The violet outline marks the next cut, the block labelled “Cut” in Section A–A. The Section A–A view looks north, so east is on the right, as in the drawing.
